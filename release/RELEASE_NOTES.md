# Tea 0.1.0 pre-alpha

This is a very early, terminal-assisted preview for technical testers. Tea is
unfinished, may break, and should not be trusted with irreplaceable data.

## Before downloading

- This build supports Apple Silicon Macs only.
- It requires Homebrew, `uv`, and Ollama; setup steps are included.
- It is not signed or notarised. macOS may require you to Control-click the
  launchers and choose **Open**.
- Tea is intended for adults and may support explicit content.

The download contains Tea and clean starter configuration, but not Ollama,
ComfyUI, model weights, voice, or optional image and motion adapters. Chat works
with a local Ollama model. Images and video can optionally use your own RunPod
account; provider charges are paid directly to that provider.

Tea requires no Tea account or subscription. Conversations, settings, and
generated media stay in the extracted Tea folder.

Download the ZIP and follow `README.txt` inside it. The `.sha256` file can be
used to verify the archive before opening it.
